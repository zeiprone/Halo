A native macOS companion for Spotify

Music, seen differently.

Halo gives your listening a visual pulse: time-synced lyrics, a notch player, and ambient light that follows the music.

01 / LYRICS

Every word, right on cue.

Lyrics are fetched from LRCLIB and displayed in sync with Spotify playback, in a focused overlay or full editorial view.

02 / CONTROL

At home in the notch.

A compact player rests at the MacBook notch. Hover for controls, then get back to what you were doing.

03 / ATMOSPHERE

Light that listens.

Optional audio-reactive ambient lighting gives the edge of every connected display a quiet, responsive glow.

Built in the open

Your music. Your Mac. Your control.

Halo is MIT licensed, private by design, and built with SwiftUI and AppKit. It has no account, analytics, backend, audio upload, or recording. Contributions are welcome.
